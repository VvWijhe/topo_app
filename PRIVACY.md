# Privacy policy — Lodestar

*Last updated: 26 October 2026*

Lodestar is a geography learning app made by an independent developer in the Netherlands.

## What the app stores
- **Your progress, points and settings** are stored **only on your own device**. They never leave your phone.
- The app contains **no ads, no analytics and no tracking**, and does not ask for your location, contacts, camera or any other permission.

## Online ranking
The daily challenge has an online ranking, run on Google Firebase (servers in the EU). When you finish a daily challenge, the app sends:
- a random player ID (created by Firebase anonymous sign-in; not linked to your name, email or phone),
- the nickname you choose yourself (optional),
- your result: points, countries found, mistakes, hints, time and date.

Other players can see your nickname and result in the ranking. Nothing else is sent. You can delete your online results in the app under Settings → Ranking → Delete my online data.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

## Contact
Questions? Use the contact email address shown on the app's Google Play page.
