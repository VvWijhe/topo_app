# Privacy policy — Lodestar

*Last updated: 25 October 2026*

Lodestar is a geography learning app made by an independent developer in the Netherlands.

## What the app stores
- **Your progress, points and settings** are stored **only on your own device**. They never leave your phone.
- The app contains **no ads, no analytics and no tracking**, and does not ask for your location, contacts, camera or any other permission.

## Online ranking (not active yet)
The app has a ranking for the daily challenge. At the moment it works **offline only**. When the online ranking is switched on, this policy will be updated before that version is released. It will then send:
- a random player ID (not linked to your name, email or phone),
- the nickname you choose yourself,
- your daily challenge result (points, countries found, time, date).

You will be able to delete this data from within the app.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

## Contact
Questions? Use the contact email address shown on the app's Google Play page.
